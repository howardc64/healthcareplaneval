Health Plan Evaluator

1. Household annual health needs

Enter what you expect to use in a typical year. Medication counts are annual fills (a monthly prescription is 12).

2. Health plans

Add one card per plan. All dollar figures are annual household amounts unless labelled otherwise (deductible and max out-of-pocket are the family amounts). Service prices are what you pay per item for a copay, or the allowed price that the deductible and coinsurance apply to.

3. Stress scenario

A bad year layered on top of normal use. Counts apply to each selected person (select both to double them). Imaging counts are per ER visit.

HSA limits and rules (editable)

Defaults are the IRS 2027 figures (Rev. Proc. 2026-24) for family HDHP coverage. Update them for other years.

Results

Green cells mark the lowest cost in each comparison row. "With deductible" applies the deductible, your coinsurance share and the max out-of-pocket cap. "No deductible limit" is every listed price paid in full. Estimates only, not tax, financial or insurance advice.
