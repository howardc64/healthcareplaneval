Health Plan Evaluator

1. Household annual health needs

Enter what you expect to use in a typical year. Medication counts are annual fills (a monthly prescription is 12).

2. Costs

Your estimate of the allowed price per unit for each service. The deductible and coinsurance are applied to these prices for every plan. A price entered under a plan's Evaluator prices overrides it for that plan; if both are blank the app uses the plan's copay amount.

3. Stress scenario

A bad year layered on top of normal use. Counts apply to each selected person (select both to double them). Blood work, X-ray and imaging counts are per ER visit.

4. Health plans

Add one card per plan. Tick Hide in a plan's upper right corner to collapse the whole plan to its name. Every field of health-plan.schema.json is on the card, grouped into sections; tick Hide on a section to fold it away (sections that do not affect the cost start hidden). Each service has its own cost share: a flat copay or a coinsurance percentage, and whether the deductible applies first. Deductible and max out-of-pocket are the family amounts. Prices come from section 2 (Costs); a price under a plan's Evaluator prices overrides it for that plan, and with neither the app uses the copay amount.

Have an LLM fill health-plan.schema.json from a plan summary PDF (prompt: fill-health-plan-prompt.md), then load the result here. Loaded plans are added to your list.
HSA limits and rules (editable)

Defaults are the IRS 2027 figures (Rev. Proc. 2026-24) for family HDHP coverage. Update them for other years.

Results

Green cells mark the lowest cost in each comparison row. "With deductible" applies each service's own copay or coinsurance, the deductible (only to services where it applies) and the max out-of-pocket cap; services that are not covered are paid in full. "No deductible limit" is every listed price paid in full. Estimates only, not tax, financial or insurance advice.

Line-item detail

Every service and medication in your annual needs for one plan: the base (allowed) cost, how the plan covers it, how much of the deductible it uses, what you pay and what the insurer pays. Each row is one kind of service with its number of units; the deductible is shared across rows in proportion to their allowed price, as in the results.
